
Dynamic Delivery

* Animate Points Individually: Use simple "Fade" or "Appear" animations to reveal bullets one by one. This prevents the audience from reading ahead and keeps them focused on what you are currently saying.
* Dim Prior Points: Consider using "dimming" animations that gray out previous bullets as you move to the next one to maintain focus on the current topic. [2, 4, 8, 14] 

Better Alternatives
When possible, replace standard lists with more engaging visuals:

* Icons & Shapes: Use theme-related icons or place text inside shapes (like arrows or boxes) instead of plain black dots.
* SmartArt: Convert lists into diagrams to show relationships or processes more clearly.
* Full Imagery: Replace a list of 6 bullets with 6 separate slides, each featuring one key point and a high-quality background image. [15, 16, 17, 18, 19]
